How this review works

The analyser reads your CV the way a senior engineer who is hiring does: skimming for evidence, not adjectives. It scores six areas, then tells you the good and the bad without sugar-coating.

  • Impact & metrics (25) — numbers and outcomes
  • Technical depth (20) — stack, depth signals, evidence
  • Language (15), Structure/ATS (15), Credibility (15), Presentation (10)

It's a rules-based review, not a human or an LLM — it can't verify your claims and it can miss nuance. For a deeper read, use the optional AI review below the results.
